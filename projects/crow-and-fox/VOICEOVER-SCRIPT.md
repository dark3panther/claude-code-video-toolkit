# The Crow and the Fox: voiceover script

Grade 2 English learners. Narrator = calm, slow (Kokoro `af_heart`, speed 0.7);
Fox = `am_michael` (0.76); Crow = `bf_emma` (0.76). Source of truth: `scripts/gen_audio.py`.

1. **Title** (Narrator): The Crow and the Fox.
2. **Cheese** (Narrator): One morning, a crow found a piece of cheese. She flew up into a tall tree.
3. **Fox** (Narrator): A hungry fox walked by. He saw the cheese.
   (Fox) "I want that cheese!" (Narrator) he said.
4. **Flatter** (Fox) "Hello, Crow!" (Narrator) said the Fox.
   (Fox) "Your feathers are so shiny. I think your voice is beautiful too. Please sing for me!"
5. **Sing** (Narrator): The Crow felt very proud. She opened her beak and sang, (Crow) "Caw! Caw!"
6. **Fall** (Narrator): The cheese fell down. The Fox caught it and ran away.
   (Fox) "Thank you!" (Narrator) he laughed.
7. **Moral** (Narrator): Moral: Be careful when someone says nice things just to get something from you.
8. **Key words** (Narrator reads each as its card appears): crow, fox, cheese, tree, feathers, beak, sing.
