# The Boy with the Whispering Pocket: performance script

Pilot for the Reading Buddy story videos (pixel-art style of *The Crow and the Fox*). Grade 2 English
learners, so the words are exactly the story's words; the acting carries the emotion. Recorded with
ElevenLabs `eleven_v4`, whose bracketed tags direct the delivery and are not spoken.

## Cast

| Role | Voice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Narrator | Katie, expressive audiobook storyteller (American) | Warm and clear for young learners, with a wide emotional range for suspense and wonder. Matches the app's American voice. |
| Leo, 7 | Teddy Twinkle, cartoon boy (American) | Bright, small, eager; instantly a child, never confused with the narrator. |
| Mrs. Lin, older teacher | Grandma Muffins, soft and kind (American) | Gentle, articulate, a little slower and lower: calm authority and delight. |

Voices stay the same for these characters in every story they appear in.

## Story arc

Curiosity (a glowing pebble) → playful mystery (the pocket hums) → a game of hot-and-cold → discovery
(a secret!) → wonder (letters from fifty years ago) → warm, satisfied ending. The emotional peak is the
time capsule; the quietest moment is the last line.
